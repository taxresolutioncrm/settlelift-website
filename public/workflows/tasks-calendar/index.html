01 / The work

Turn the next step into scheduled work.

Enrollment reviews, creditor callbacks and document follow-ups have different owners and dates. SettleLift’s prototype links appointments to the client and to a follow-up task, keeping work visible beyond a calendar square.

02 / The process

How the work connects.

  1. Choose a calendar date to prepare an appointment and prefill the selected day. Link the synthetic client and record the purpose.
  2. Save an appointment with its related task and demo alert. Review unread notices separately from completed tasks.
  3. Cancel the appointment when plans change and close its linked task. Keep the case context available for rescheduling.
03 / Context & decisions

The details that matter.

Reminder preferences are currently saved values, not scheduled deliveries. Real reminders require tenant-aware time zones, a server worker, the correct recipient identity and provider verification. In production, cancellation and rescheduling must update the worker’s pending jobs as well as the visible calendar. The current local calendar is intended to test that workflow, not to replace a connected scheduling service.

05 / Common question

Will choosing a reminder time send an alert?

Not in the current preview. A preference remains local until a verified server scheduling and delivery service is connected.
