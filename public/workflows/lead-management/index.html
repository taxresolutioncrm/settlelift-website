01 / The work

Give every inquiry a next action.

A lead pipeline is useful only when an inquiry creates accountable work. The SettleLift prototype keeps a lead’s source, state, assignment context and follow-up together, and makes possible duplicates visible before conversion.

02 / The process

A lead handoff that keeps its context.

  1. Record the incoming inquiry and its source. Review possible matches without silently overwriting an existing person.
  2. Create the task, unread notification and unsent draft alongside the lead so a missing provider connection is visible.
  3. Convert after review. Preserve the original follow-up links and return the existing client if the same conversion is retried.
03 / Context & decisions

What stays connected

Keep the inquiry source, duplicate decision and assigned next action through conversion. The intended workflow creates a task, notice and draft together; live website capture and delivered email still require their own verified connections.
