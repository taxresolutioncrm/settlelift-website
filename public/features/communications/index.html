01 / The work

The conversation belongs with the case.

A follow-up can begin as a call, continue by email and end with a document request. When those activities live separately, it becomes harder to see who owes the next action. SettleLift is being built around client-linked communication and visible pending work.

02 / The process

From the conversation to the next action.

  1. Prepare a client-linked email, SMS or fax draft with an editable template. Immediate local autosave helps preserve work while navigating the preview.
  2. Create appointments with linked tasks and demo alerts. Keep reminders and the assigned case visible, and distinguish an unread notice from a completed follow-up.
  3. Use the creditor directory to identify the team, phone extension and contact hours before making an authorized call through a future connected provider. Record outcomes against the relevant account.
03 / Context & decisions

The details that matter.

The lead workflow creates an alert, task and unsent draft together. The prepared backend records a blocked delivery event until a provider is configured, so a database insert is not mistaken for a delivered email. Live voice, SMS, email, inbound/outbound fax, voicemail and scheduled reminder delivery require separate provider wiring and verification. Browser room chat and unread badges currently demonstrate the experience locally.

04 / Product status

What you can explore today.

Connection work remaining

05 / Common question

Does saving a draft send the message?

No. The preview stores drafts locally and sends nothing. A future live send must use the configured company identity, delivery status and retry-safe server workflow.
