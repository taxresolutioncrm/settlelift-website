01 / The work

Make the next release reviewable.

Implementation is more than putting a login page on a domain. The team needs the correct company structure, secure identities, trustworthy data and verified provider behavior. SettleLift separates its development preview from the connected release those requirements will support.

02 / The process

A staged path to a connected release.

  1. Map your operation: offices, staff roles, client stages and the work that must move between teams.
  2. Review source data and files, provider requirements, company branding and the access each role needs.
  3. Plan training, confirm workflow acceptance and verify connected services before customer onboarding.
03 / Context & decisions

What stays connected

A controlled move starts with a clear inventory: client records, debt accounts, supporting files, open offers and active follow-ups. Review how each item maps into the new workspace, who validates it and how the team will reconcile the result before relying on it.
