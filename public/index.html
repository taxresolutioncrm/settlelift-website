Questions, answered

A few things worth knowing.

Can I use this with real client data today?

No. The current preview is a private prototype for synthetic data. Real authentication, shared storage, providers, and production controls must be connected and verified first.

Is it designed for multiple offices or companies?

Yes. The planned architecture uses one product project with isolated company tenants and role- or case-scoped access. The prepared isolation controls still need verification in the real project before launch.

Will there be a Spanish interface?

Yes. The workspace preview includes an English/Spanish toggle. Client names, notes, and document content stay in their original language rather than being silently translated.

Does SettleLift hold or move consumer funds?

The prototype does not hold or move funds. Its planner uses hypothetical inputs and does not verify bank accounts. Future financial integrations require configured providers, authorization, and reconciliation.

Where can I see pricing?

Commercial pricing has not been published. Terms will be provided before any paid onboarding; this preview does not collect cards or activate subscriptions.
