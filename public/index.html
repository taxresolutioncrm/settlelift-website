BEFORE WE TALK

Good questions. Clear answers.

All product questions ↗
Can we use SettleLift with real clients today?

SettleLift provides access through authorized workspaces. Public examples use synthetic data. Confirm your office's configuration and integration requirements with our team.

Is Connect already a live creditor network?

No. It demonstrates a directory and collaboration approach. Invited creditor portals, direct APIs and payment networks are separate connections and are not active here.

Can we discuss our current CRM and migration?

Yes. Contact SettleLift with your company, current tools and the workflows you want to improve. Pricing and launch availability have not been announced.
