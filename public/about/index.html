01 / The work

Built around the work between the stages.

SettleLift is a RomyLabs product in development focused on debt settlement operations. The goal is a workspace where the people, creditor relationships, documents and next actions remain connected throughout the case. The approved rising-wing identity expresses that direction without promising a consumer outcome.

02 / The process

A product shaped around settlement operations.

  1. Make the case understandable: one person may have several debts, changing collectors and multiple proposals. Preserve those relationships.
  2. Make decisions explainable: show missing evidence, expiration dates and funding assumptions beside the next action.
  3. Make access deliberate: give employees, clients and invited representatives only the view appropriate to their role and case.
03 / Context & decisions

The details that matter.

The product combines an English/Spanish interface, a creditor operations hub and scenario planning with shared CRM foundations such as tasks, documents and communication drafts. Its differentiation is the intended connection between these workflows, not an unsupported claim of being the only platform with a portal. The current preview uses synthetic data and does not advertise proven savings, fabricated testimonials, customer counts or certified integrations.

05 / Common question

Is SettleLift a debt relief company?

This website describes software being developed for debt settlement operators. It does not provide a consumer settlement service or accept payments toward a consumer debt.
